PassGen Keyboard is a minimalist, zero-permission Android Input Method (IME) designed exclusively for generating cryptographically secure passwords on the fly.

Features:
* Zero permissions: No internet access, no storage access, no tracking or ads.
* Customizable character sets: lowercase (a-z), uppercase (A-Z), digits (0-9), and special symbols (!@#$%^&*).
* Adjustable password length (from 4 to 64 characters) with guaranteed inclusion of at least one character from each selected set.
* Two large action buttons: "Generate" (types directly into the active field) and "Generate & Copy" (types into the field and copies to the clipboard with sensitive content masking on Android 13+).
* Built with zero external dependencies for minimal attack surface and tiny APK size (~50 KB).
* Localized in English, Russian, and Kazakh.
